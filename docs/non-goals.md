# Non-Goals and Boundaries

This document prevents scope creep and keeps execution focused.

## Not Targeted in MVP

- Full Jira feature parity
- Enterprise-only compliance packs
- Deep portfolio/program management
- Built-in chat/video collaboration
- Multi-region distributed architecture
- Native mobile apps

## Product Principles

- Prioritize speed-to-value over feature breadth.
- Prefer simple defaults with extensibility later.
- Keep self-hosting straightforward and well documented.
- Maintain local-first capability for desktop users.
